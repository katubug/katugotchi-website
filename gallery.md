# Tama Labo Sticker Scans <a name="tama-labo-sticker-scans"></a>

For some reason, I became obsessed with buying these silly stickers so I could scan and print my own stickers to use without feeling like I was "ruining" a collectible. These have been upscaled using waifu2x, noise-removal'd in Adobe Lightroom, and then manually edited for quality and consistency. They are not 100% accurate to the originals as a result, but will produce better prints than direct scans would have.
