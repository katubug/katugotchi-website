It's so funny that since my first blog post detailing the death of my first tama in living memory, I've had so many more die in the following month or so. After my Gen 1 Mimitchi's death over the weekend, I had put the batteries back into my Paradise and had raised - hold on, I have to look this up - Tenatchi. I was going for Namakemotchi but I was just *too good* apparently.

And then I put the tama in my purse and completely forgot that I was running it for 48 hours straight. As you can imagine, my poor green monkey boy did not survive the process. So now I'm going for Ananatchi, or maybe Tropical Potsunentchi.

Also in tama news, I got my hands on a Mushi Pet! I haven't taken a photo yet, but here's the model I got:

<img src="/images/mushipet_gaycatgirl.png">

(image from /u/gaycatgirl on reddit)

I have not put batteries in it, and I'm not certain I will. A note arrived with the package warning me that the legs are notoriously breakable, and I don't want to risk damaging it. And [this reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/tamagotchi/comments/mk63yh/finally_got_a_mushi_pet_heres_an_imgur_album_with/) says that it's not all that fun to play. Still, maybe.

It was $80 on eBay, and it was admittedly an impulse purchase that I did not really do my research on. I could probably have gotten a better price, but I had had a good day at my market and wanted to buy myself a treat, and this particular model has been something I wanted for ages, so I snatched it up. No regrets, tbh.
