title: "actually playing with my collection"
---

So, I feel like collecting virtual pets and playing with them are two different hobbies. Much like yarn collecting vs needlework, or hoarding vs junk journaling.

I don't want to fall too far into the "collecting" space, so I make it a point to run all of my tamas as much as my sanity will allow. I actually have a goal to play a full generation of each virtual pet I own, which is both exciting and somewhat daunting, given the size of my collection. And uh, how bad some of them are.

My boyfriend even recently commented that I play with my tamas more often than he'd expect a collector to. And I take that as a compliment! Toys should be played with. I think buying something (especially a rare thing) only to put it on a shelf and never touch it is a very Grown Up thing to do, and I don't much associate myself with that. It feels like dishonoring my inner child to disallow myself with playing with something just because it's valuable. You know what I mean?

Now, ideally, I would love to do a youtube series on this, but let's be real - am I gonna remember to film everything? And then when I have the footage, will I really edit it? The answer *could* be yes, but if I'm honest, I don't know how likely that is. But I think it would be fun to have a series of blind playthroughs of different devices - especially the ones that are harder to find - and then maybe a second playthrough where I'm allow to consult a guide. Just thoughts for now.
