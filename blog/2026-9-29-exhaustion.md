This isn't going to be super tamagotchi related, fair warning. But it's my blog, and therefore I say we're going to talk about perimenopause.

So let's be real. If you're reading this, there's a good chance you're also a uterus-having person of the right age to start (early) perimenopause. I'm 41, but I now think I started peri back when I was 38. I didn't catch it, because I didn't realize what a LAUNDRY LIST of SYMPTOMS it could have. And doctors, by and large, know absolutely dick all about it.

It was this year that I started getting hot flashes, and after being basically snubbed by my doctor, started doing my own research so I could advocate for myself. Which, unfortunately, really seems to be a necessary thing, these days. For me, since I'm chronically ill, I have always had to be proactive about my health. But it's much worse, now. Lemme add some more commas to this paragraph, perhaps,,,,,,

So I want to suggest to you, if you have a uterus and are of a certain age (remember that PCOS and ADHD are both associated with early perimenopause!), that you take a look at (this menopause wiki article)[https://menopausewiki.ca/is-this-perimenopause/] and just...see. If you have no symptoms, great, you're all good. But if you do, well, then I'm afraid that's where the hard part starts: finding care. I can't find you a doctor who knows about peri and will listen, but I will say that the community at (/r/perimenopause)[https://www.reddit.com/r/Perimenopause/about/] has been actually saving my life recently. Yes, it's reddit, but it's a tiny pocket of reddit filled with people who are right where I am, and it makes me feel less alone.

Anyway. The way this relates at all to tamagotchi is the fact that I haven't started my Ocean back up, even though I intended to. Peri has drained me of all energy, given me a huge dose of irrational rage, and in general is just making me a bad tama mama. So I decided to give myself a week to rest up and regain my iron stores (GET YOUR FERRITIN CHECKED IF YOU ARE EXHAUSTED) before I dive back in.

In the meantime, I am raising a P2 that I bought at Walgreens with the "Bright Garden" shell. I was initially going to shut it off for the moment so that I could focus on the Ocean, but well... here we are. Right now I have a little teenage poop machine who I suspect I have made a care mistake with, so I guess goodbye to my Mimitchi dreams.

And seriously. Take care of yourself.
