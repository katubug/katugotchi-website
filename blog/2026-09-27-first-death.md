title: "my first dead tama"

You're not going to believe me when I say this, but for the first time in my 41 years of life, one of my tamagotchis died. Of course there's a catch - I'm not counting the times as a kid where I stopped playing it and just left it in the drawer, never to be seen again. But this is the first time I have have a tamagotchi *that I was actively playing* die on me.

It was, perhaps unsurprisingly, my Tamagotchi Ocean. My son, the jellyfish, passed away at 11:00am CST on September 27th, 2026. He succumbed to an unfortunate bear attack, which is actually the most common method of demise for jellyfish.

At the time of death he was 1 year old and weighed 39lbs. Again, this is totally normal for a jellyfish.

What can I say in eulogy?

`Oh, my son. My lovely jelly boy. You weren't the best child, but you were mine. In all honesty, I am relieved that you have passed away, because you were extremely needy and frankly annoying. However, I hope to see you again someday when I have more time to dedicate to your care. Now that I have read a guide instead of going in blind, I feel more qualified to provide that care. Was this all my fault, and totally preventable? Maybe. But enough about that. Now, you are gone. Rest in peace, small but incredibly dense jellyfish man.`
