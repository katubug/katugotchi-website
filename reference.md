# Tamagotchi Reference Websites Directory

Sep 19, 2026 · @Katu

## Summary

The essential five hubs are **Tamagotchi Wiki** (encyclopedic database), **Gotchi Garden** (care and growth charts for every era), **MrBlinky** (English patches and download catalogs), **Tama-Palace** (news) and **TamaTalk** (the long-running forum).

- **Hacking and tools:** Tama Hacks, MameMame Labs, TamaEmu, jcrona's TamaLIB/TamaTool, Natalie Silvanovich's Tamagotchi-Hack, Tiny Pocket Friends (Smart English patch), the Uni Tour Code Generator, and the Meets/On fan apps MyMeets and MixingMeets.
- **Manuals:** Tama Conservatory is the dedicated archive for virtual pet instruction manuals.
- **Repair and mods:** Marcel's Tama Spa & Repair (Germany), Mikablu's Tama Mod Shop (USA), Tamagotchi Vietnam and TamaTinkerer (USA). Tamagotchi Center has been removed from the recommended services.
- **Custom content:** Myiko, basketworm, Gatu and Shellytchi make characters, items, rooms and games you load onto a device; JellySnacks and Munina make faceplates and customs.
- **Communities:** activity has shifted to Discord (Tamagotchi Collectors, about 18,500 members) and Reddit (r/tamagotchi, about 120,000 members). Several vintage fan sites are now static or archived.

The brand passed 100 million units shipped worldwide as of July 31, 2025, which keeps new devices and fan documentation coming.

## 3. Manuals & scans

[Tama Conservatory](https://tamaconservatory.blogspot.com/) is the dedicated archive: a growing library of virtual pet instruction manuals as viewable PDFs, vintage and modern. It is run by @LilisTama and covers Tamagotchi, Digimon, Giga Pets, Nano Pets, Sanrio, Sumikko, Neopets, Pokemon, Chiikawa, Disney, Punirunes and Bitzee, browsable by series, animal, genre, feature, company or era (1990s to 2020s). It has a [manual library](https://tamaconservatory.blogspot.com/p/contents.html), a [wishlist of missing manuals](https://tamaconservatory.blogspot.com/p/missing-manual-wishlist.html) you can contribute to, and a grand tour for newcomers.

Other sources:

- **Gotchi Garden** and **Fuzzy N Chic** host downloadable manuals and care sheets (e.g. Smart, Uni and Paradise English manuals).
- **MrBlinky's site** and the official Bandai sites carry official manual content.
- **TamaTalk** members upload scans, and the **Tamagotchi Wiki** documents manual contents per device.
